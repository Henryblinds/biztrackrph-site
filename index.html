Data Deletion Request

How to delete the data BizTrackrPH stores about you

What BizTrackrPH collects

BizTrackrPH is a local-first app. Your business data — products, categories, sales, store profile, and product images — lives on your device only and is never uploaded to our servers.

We do collect a small amount of anonymous data through Google Firebase to keep the app stable:

  • Crash reports (via Firebase Crashlytics) — so we can fix bugs.
  • App usage events (via Firebase Analytics) — e.g. screen views and feature usage counts.
  • A device installation ID generated by Firebase.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or any business data (products or sales).

Option 1 Delete all data inside the app

This is the fastest way and works without contacting us. It also resets the anonymous Firebase installation ID, so future analytics events from this device cannot be linked to past activity.

  1. Open BizTrackrPH on your device.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap Erase all data…
  5. Type ERASE in the confirmation dialog and tap Erase everything.

✗ Deleted

All products, categories, sales records, store profile, backup history, and the Firebase installation ID linked to this device.

✓ Not affected

Your Google Play purchase of BizTrackrPH Pro stays linked to your Google account and can be restored anytime.

Option 2 Request server-side deletion

To request deletion of any historic anonymous analytics or crash data that may still be on Google's servers, email us. We'll process your request within 30 days.

Please send the email from the address you want us to reply to, and include any Firebase Installation ID you saved beforehand (optional — we'll do our best to locate matching records).

Uninstalling the app

Uninstalling BizTrackrPH from your device removes the on-device database, all product images, and the app's settings. However, uninstalling does not by itself delete any anonymous analytics or crash data already received by Firebase — use Option 1 before uninstalling, or send a request via Option 2.
