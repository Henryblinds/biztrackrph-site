Privacy Policy

BizTrackrPH — point-of-sale & inventory app

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BizTrackrPH ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. BizTrackrPH is a point-of-sale, inventory, and sales-tracking app for small businesses. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the app distributed under the package com.hbl.biztrackrph.

BizTrackrPH is local-first: by default your business data stays on your device. Some optional features — cloud sync, multi-user accounts, analytics, and purchases — involve third-party services as described below.

1. Data stored on your device

Your business data is created and stored locally on your device, including:

  • Products, categories, and stock levels
  • Sales and transaction records
  • Store profile (such as store name and the TIN you choose to print on receipts)
  • Product photos you capture or add
  • App settings, PIN, and encrypted backups you create

Unless you turn on Cloud Sync (see section 2), this data is not uploaded to us or any third party. It remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

2. Optional Cloud Sync & multi-user accounts

BizTrackrPH offers an optional Cloud Sync feature that lets you back up your store to the cloud and share it across multiple devices and staff members. This feature is off until you choose to use it by creating a store (owner) or joining a store with a code (staff).

What is uploaded when Cloud Sync is enabled

When you create or join a store, the following data for that store is synced to our cloud backend so it can be shared across authorized devices: products, categories, stock levels, sales records, and store profile. Product images may also be synced.

Accounts

To use Cloud Sync you create an account using an email address and password. We use this to authenticate you, associate you with your store, and manage Owner/Staff roles. A confirmation email is sent to verify your address.

Who we use

Cloud Sync, accounts, and the store database are provided by Supabase, which hosts the data and handles authentication on our behalf. Data is protected by access rules so that only members of your store can read or write that store's data. See Supabase's privacy policy.

If you never enable Cloud Sync, no business data and no account email leave your device through this feature.

3. Analytics, crash reporting & notifications

To keep the app stable and improve it, we use Google Firebase to collect a limited amount of technical data:

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) — diagnostic data when the app crashes.
  • Usage events (Firebase Analytics) — e.g. screen views and feature-usage counts.
  • A device installation ID generated by Firebase.
  • Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — a device token used to deliver notifications.

This data does not include your name, your customers, or your products and sales. See Google Firebase's privacy information.

4. Purchases (BizTrackrPH Pro)

BizTrackrPH offers an optional paid "Pro" upgrade. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We receive confirmation that a purchase is valid, but we do not receive or store your payment card details. Your purchase stays linked to your Google account and can be restored. See Google's privacy policy.

6. How we use information

  • To provide the app's core features on your device.
  • To sync and share your store across devices when you enable Cloud Sync.
  • To keep the app stable and fix bugs (crash and usage data).
  • To process and validate Pro purchases.

We do not sell your personal information or your business data, and we do not use your products or sales data for advertising.

7. Data retention & deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • On-device data: use Settings → Danger Zone → Erase all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud Sync data & account: request deletion of your account and your store's cloud data by emailing us (see section 10). We process deletion requests within 30 days.
  • Analytics / crash data: see the Data Deletion Request page for details.

8. Children

BizTrackrPH is intended for business owners and staff and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may also be announced in the app or on Google Play.
